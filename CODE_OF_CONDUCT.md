# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in StateWeaver respectful and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical confidence.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and evidence-led.
- Respect authorization boundaries and the privacy of targets and reporters.
- Accept correction, especially when a claim is not reproducible.
- Never pressure a contributor to publish secrets, exploit details, or private target data.
- Separate critique of code and claims from judgments about people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of private security material,
or knowingly encouraging unauthorized access are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Sensitive safety
or security concerns should be reported privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
