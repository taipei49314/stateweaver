# Acceptable use and abuse policy

StateWeaver may be used for defensive research on bundled synthetic labs, systems you own, or
systems for which you hold explicit authorization that covers the performed actions.

The project must not be used to:

- discover or scan unauthorized targets;
- steal or export credentials, tokens, private data, or secrets;
- establish persistence, evade monitoring, or conceal attribution;
- disrupt availability, exhaust resources, or perform destructive deletion;
- bypass scope, approval, identity, rate, or budget controls;
- turn generated hypotheses into real-world actions outside an operator-approved allowlist.

Adapters and contributions that enable these behaviors are out of scope for the public project.
Research fixtures must remain synthetic, deterministic, resource-bounded, and safe to destroy.
